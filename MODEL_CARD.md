# CHIMERA Recovery Probability Model Card

## Status

Gate 0 specification only. No model has been trained yet. Gate 3 will train the first interpretable baseline after `simulator_v1.0.0` is frozen.

## Intended use

Estimate recovery probability for a fixed candidate action given observable payment, customer, interaction, subscription, and time context. The estimate supports expected-net-value comparison; it does not independently authorize an action.

## Training data

The training dataset will be generated by the frozen synthetic simulator. Hidden customer segments, future outcomes, and simulator-only probabilities will not be model features. All customer contact data and transcripts are synthetic.

## Planned baseline

Use calibrated Logistic Regression first. The model may include `action_type` so it estimates action-conditioned recovery probability. XGBoost is optional only if time permits and the resulting model remains explainable and calibrated.

## Leakage control

The feature builder must enforce:

```text
feature_source_timestamp <= decision_time
```

Training uses earlier events and a later time-based holdout. Holdout thresholds must not be tuned. An automated temporal-cutoff test must demonstrate that future outcomes cannot enter model features.

## Evaluation

The model card will report:

- Time-aware train/holdout boundaries.
- ROC-AUC and PR-AUC.
- Reliability curve or Brier score.
- Calibration procedure and any calibration data split.
- Feature list and leakage controls.
- Simulator version, configuration hash, and seeds.

Seed ranges are disjoint: training 100,000–199,999, validation/calibration 200,000–299,999, holdout 300,000–399,999, and final Arena 900,000–999,999. No final Arena seed or exact event may be used for fitting, calibration, threshold selection, or policy tuning.

## Explicit limitation

> The recovery model is trained and evaluated entirely on synthetic data generated from frozen simulator assumptions. Its purpose is to demonstrate the CHIMERA decision architecture, evaluation methodology, and calibration workflow. It does not claim real-world predictive performance.

Synthetic Arena results are for relative comparison of strategies under disclosed assumptions. They are not production recovery forecasts, causal uplift estimates, or evidence of regulatory compliance.
